🛡 Privacy Policy

Last Updated & Effective: November 13, 2026

🔒
Zero-Cloud Storage Architecture:
GPSnap operates on an on-device local processing model. We do not operate remote servers to collect, upload, store, or sell your photos, videos, audio tracks, or GPS tracks. Everything you snap stays on your phone.

1. Introduction

Welcome to GPSnap: GPS Map Camera & Geotag Timestamp (referred to as "GPSnap", "we", "our", or "us"). We provide professional field photography and geostamping tools for engineers, surveyors, delivery workers, inspectors, travelers, and enthusiasts. We understand the vital importance of data privacy and are committed to complete transparency regarding how your data is accessed, processed, and safeguarded.

2. Permissions & Data We Access

To function as a high-precision GPS camera, GPSnap requests explicit user permissions on Android. We strictly adhere to the principle of data minimization:

  • Precise & Approximate Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): Required in real time to calculate your GPS coordinates (latitude, longitude, altitude, accuracy, speed, and compass heading). This allows the app to render the geotag stamp onto your media, retrieve reverse-geocoded street addresses, fetch ambient weather, and show mini-map snapshots.
  • Camera (CAMERA): Required to show the live camera preview, adjust exposure/focus, and capture photos and videos with live timestamp watermarks.
  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Accessed strictly when you record a video with sound enabled. We never record audio in the background or during photo capture mode.
  • Storage & Media Access (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Required to save stamped photos, videos, and PDF field reports to your device storage (via Android Scoped Storage / MediaStore API), display your past captures in the built-in gallery, and allow you to select a custom brand logo from your gallery.
  • Network Access (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Required to download map tiles (OpenStreetMap & Google Maps), convert coordinates to human-readable addresses, fetch live weather/AQI, load sponsor advertisements via Google AdMob, and check for official updates via Google Play In-App Updates.
  • Device Accounts (GET_ACCOUNTS): Utilized solely if you explicitly choose to link your Google account for optional Google Drive cloud synchronization.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE): Powers optional haptic shutter feedback on photo capture.

3. Strict Foreground-Only Location Policy

📍
No Background Tracking: GPSnap does NOT request the Android ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION permission. GPS hardware polling activates only while the app is actively open on your screen and stops the instant you minimize or close the app.

5. Advertising Controls & Privacy Choices

GPSnap displays ads via Google AdMob. Depending on your region, Google AdMob and its advertising partners may use device identifiers (like the Google Advertising ID / AAID) to serve personalized or non-personalized ads.

  • GDPR / European Consent: For users located in the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, and Switzerland, GPSnap displays a Google Certified Consent Management Platform (CMP / UMP) dialog on launch, allowing you to grant, customize, or refuse advertising consent.
  • Resetting Your Advertising ID: You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID at any time in Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

6. Data Storage, Security & Retention

All your captured photos, video recordings, generated PDF reports, customized logo files, and app settings are stored locally on your device hardware (e.g. within Pictures/GPSnap, Movies/GPSnap, and Documents/GPSnap).

Because we do not maintain remote cloud servers for user data, we retain zero personal files or location trajectories on external systems. Network communications for map tiles, weather, and geocoding utilize secure encrypted HTTPS channels.

7. Global Privacy Frameworks & Your Rights

We honor privacy rights established under global regulations:

  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR - EEA/UK): You have the right to access, rectify, port, restrict, or erase personal data, object to processing, and withdraw consent. Legal bases: contractual necessity (app service), explicit consent (personalized ads), and legitimate interest (anti-fraud and app stability). You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local Data Protection Authority.
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA): California residents have the right to know what personal info is collected, request deletion, and opt out of sharing. GPSnap does not sell personal information.
  • India Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP 2023): Data processing is conducted lawfully and transparently. For grievances, contact our grievance desk at contact@aniket.one.

8. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA)

GPSnap is an enterprise, utility, and documentation tool not intended for children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in the European Union). We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children. If you believe a minor has provided data, contact us at contact@aniket.one for immediate removal.

🗑 Data Deletion & Contact

Because GPSnap does not host your photos or location trajectories on remote cloud servers, you have full control over your data directly on your device:

1. Deleting Media & Captured Records

  • Open the in-app GPSnap Gallery or your phone's native Google Photos / Files app.
  • Select the photos, videos, or PDF reports you wish to remove and tap Delete.

2. Resetting App Preferences & Cached Data

  • Open your phone's Settings → Apps → GPSnap.
  • Tap Storage & cache.
  • Tap Clear Cache and Clear Storage. This resets all local custom stamp settings and cached reverse-geocoded data.

3. Revoking System Permissions

  • Navigate to Settings → Apps → GPSnap → Permissions.
  • You can freely revoke Location, Camera, Audio, or Storage permissions at any time.
