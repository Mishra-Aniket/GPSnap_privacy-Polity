# 01 // ARCHITECTURAL PRINCIPLE

Zero-Cloud On-Device Local Processing

GPSnap: GPS Map Camera & Geotag Timestamp (referred to as "GPSnap", "the App", "we", "our", or "us") is engineered strictly around a local-first processing model. We do not operate, license, or maintain remote cloud servers to collect, upload, analyze, or monetize your captured photos, recorded videos, audio tracks, or GPS coordinates.

All image capture, optical rendering, geotag watermark burning, barometric altitude calculation, azimuth compass alignment, and PDF field inspection report generation are executed entirely inside your device's local application sandbox.

# 02 // SENSITIVE RUNTIME PERMISSIONS

Permissions & Explicit Functional Purpose

GPSnap requests runtime permissions under Google Play Developer Policy guidelines strictly when necessary to deliver core camera stamping functionality:

  • Precise & Approximate Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): Queried strictly in the foreground while the camera is active to calculate your real-time coordinates, altitude, heading, and speed, embed stamps onto your captures, and query reverse geocoding for street addresses.
  • Camera (CAMERA): Powers the live viewfinder preview, automatic and manual exposure/focus, and captures photos and videos with real-time on-screen stamp overlays.
  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Captures audio tracks exclusively when you explicitly record a video with sound enabled. Microphone access is never initialized during photo capture mode.
  • Storage & Scoped Media (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Saves stamped photos, videos, and generated PDF reports to your device storage via Android Scoped Storage / MediaStore API, and allows selecting custom logo watermarks from your gallery.
  • Network Access (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Downloads map tiles (OpenStreetMap & Google Maps), converts coordinates into human-readable addresses via reverse geocoding, fetches ambient weather and Air Quality Index (AQI) values, and loads sponsor advertisements via Google AdMob.
  • Device Accounts (GET_ACCOUNTS): Utilized solely if you explicitly choose to authenticate your personal Google account for optional Google Drive backup sync.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE): Provides tactile shutter feedback.
# 03 // FOREGROUND POLICY

Foreground-Only Location Guarantee

GPSnap strictly does NOT request or declare the ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION permission. GPS hardware polling activates only while the app is actively on your screen. The moment you switch apps, minimize, or lock your phone, all location polling is immediately terminated.

# 01 // LOCAL DEVICE REMOVAL

Step-by-Step Device Data Deletion

  • Delete Photos & Videos: Open the in-app GPSnap Gallery or your native Google Photos / Files app, select media, and tap Delete.
  • Clear App Storage & Preferences: Open Android Settings → Apps → GPSnap → Storage & cache → Clear Storage. This permanently resets all custom stamp templates, cached addresses, and local preferences.
  • Full Application Uninstallation: Uninstalling GPSnap purges all sandboxed application files from your device.
