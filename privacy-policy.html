Zero-Cloud Local Storage Promise: GPSnap operates on a local-first processing architecture. We do not operate remote servers to collect, upload, store, or sell your photos, videos, notes, or GPS trajectories.

1. Overview & Commitment to Privacy

This Privacy Policy explains how GPSnap ("we", "our", or "us") accesses, processes, and protects your information when you use our Android mobile application. GPSnap is designed for professionals and individuals who need accurate geolocation and timestamp overlays on their photography. We respect your privacy and design our software to process media locally on your device.

2. Sensitive Permissions & Purpose Justification

To deliver real-time camera stamping and field documentation, GPSnap utilizes the following Android runtime permissions:

  • Precise & Approximate Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): Used strictly in the foreground while the camera/app is open to retrieve your GPS coordinates, altitude, compass bearing, speed, and accuracy. This data is burned into your photo/video stamps, used to look up street addresses, fetch local weather, and display mini-map snapshots.
  • Camera (CAMERA): Used to provide the live camera viewfinder, adjust focus/exposure, and capture photos and video recordings.
  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Used exclusively when you explicitly record video with audio. Never accessed during photo snapping or in the background.
  • Storage & Media Access (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Used to save stamped media and PDF inspection reports to your device's shared storage via Android Scoped Storage / MediaStore API, show past captures in the app gallery, and allow custom logo watermarks.
  • Network Access (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used to download map tiles, convert coordinates to street addresses, fetch current weather and air quality (AQI) from Open-Meteo, load sponsor ads via Google AdMob, and check for app updates.
  • Device Accounts (GET_ACCOUNTS): Used solely if you choose to connect a Google account for optional Google Drive backup sync.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE): Used for optional tactile shutter feedback.

3. Foreground-Only Location Tracking

No Background Location Services: GPSnap does not request or use ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION. Location hardware polling stops immediately when you navigate away from the camera screen or close the app.

4. Third-Party SDKs & Services

GPSnap integrates trusted third-party SDKs that operate under their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services (Location & Maps SDK): For fused GPS location and map views. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Google AdMob: For displaying sponsor advertisements. AdMob may process your device's Advertising ID, IP address, and interaction events. (AdMob Policies)
  • Google ML Kit (On-Device OCR): Processes camera frames 100% locally on your phone hardware for landmark and address recognition. Zero frames or recognized text are sent to Google cloud servers.
  • OpenStreetMap & Nominatim: Provides map tiles and reverse geocoding lookups. (OSMF Privacy Policy)
  • Open-Meteo: Provides localized weather and Air Quality Index (AQI) based strictly on anonymous coordinates. (Open-Meteo Terms)
  • Google Play In-App Updates: Ensures users receive seamless updates via Google Play.

5. Advertising Controls & User Choices

Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, and Switzerland are presented with a Google-certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent dialog to accept or customize ad personalization. You can also reset or opt out of personalized ads at any time via Android System Settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

6. Data Storage & Deletion

All photos, videos, PDF reports, and preferences reside locally on your device. We do not store or retain your files on any external server.

How to Delete Data:

  • Delete files directly from the in-app Gallery or your device's Files/Photos app.
  • Clear app data and preferences via Android Settings → Apps → GPSnap → Storage → Clear Storage.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all local caches and sandboxed settings.
  • To request deletion of any support email correspondence, contact contact@aniket.one. All requests are permanently resolved within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

GPSnap is not directed to children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in the EU). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. International Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

We comply with GDPR and CCPA/CPRA rights. We do not sell personal information. European users have rights of access, rectification, erasure, and data portability.
